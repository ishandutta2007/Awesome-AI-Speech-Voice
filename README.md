VibeVoice-ASR processes 60 minutes of audio in a single pass with structured output: Who (speaker), When (timestamps), What (content) . VibeVoice-TTS synthesizes up to 90 minutes of multi-speaker conversational audio with consistent speaker identity across the entire output . Core innovation: continuous speech tokenizers operating at 7.5 Hz ultra-low frame rate with next-token diffusion framework . Model zoo: Streaming-0.5B (real-time, single speaker), 1.5B (~90 min, 4 speakers), 7B (~45 min, 4 speakers) . Fine-tuning supported for adapting to new languages or voices . Safety features: audible AI disclaimer, imperceptible watermarking, and inference logging added after misuse incidents .
